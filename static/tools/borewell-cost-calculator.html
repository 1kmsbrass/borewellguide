2. Different rates at depth ranges (optional)

Turn this on only if the contractor’s written quote uses slabs. Each slab rate applies to the feet entered for that slab. The slab depths must add up to the total depth.

3. Additional costs (optional)

Enter only costs that are not already included in the drilling rate. Leave unknown costs at zero and replace them when you receive a quote.

How to use this estimate

For a single rate, the basic charge is total depth multiplied by the quoted rate per foot. For rate slabs, calculate each slab separately and add the results. Additional charges are then added only if they are outside the quoted drilling charge.

Estimated total = drilling charge + extra costs + entered tax/percentage

This tool performs arithmetic only. It cannot predict groundwater, recommend a safe drilling depth, validate a contractor’s quote, or guarantee that water will be found. Confirm what each charge includes and whether tax applies before relying on the total.
