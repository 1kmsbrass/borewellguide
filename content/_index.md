Borewell Guide helps homeowners, farmers and property owners understand the decisions involved in planning a borewell.

A borewell is a site-specific project. Local geology, nearby wells, seasonal groundwater conditions, access for the rig and the intended water use all matter. No website can guarantee water at a particular depth.

## Start with the essentials

- **Assess the site:** collect local information and ask qualified local professionals about the site.
- **Compare contractors:** ask about the rig, drilling method, pricing basis, casing, flushing and what the quote includes.
- **Plan the full cost:** drilling is only one part of the budget; pump, piping, electrical work and other site requirements may add to it.

**Important:** Information on this website is general guidance, not a groundwater survey or a guarantee of drilling success. Confirm site-specific decisions with qualified local professionals and relevant authorities.
